# A concrete body-integration conversation

HUGO separates persistent identity and governed intelligence from the body that
provides physical capabilities. The engineering question for a robotics partner
is narrow: can a documented platform become another authorized BodyAdapter
without moving trust, identity or safety authority into vendor transport code?

A useful proof of concept would expose a small versioned capability set,
preserve the platform's local safety controller, exercise approval/cancellation,
and prove identity continuity. SDK/API documentation, a developer unit and an
engineering contact would make that test concrete.

This showcase demonstrates software contracts with a simulated body. Historical
LOOI acceptance is recorded separately in the source evidence. A physical partner
demonstration requires new explicit hardware access, repeatable tests and a
separate reviewed video. General autonomy and arbitrary-device control are not
claimed. No outreach message is sent by this repository.
